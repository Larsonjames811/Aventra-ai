What this website collects

Very little. This site has no forms, no logins, no analytics and no advertising cookies. Our own code sets no cookies at all.

When a page loads, a few providers receive your IP address and basic browser details because they deliver the page:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and Cloudflare sits in front of it. Both receive your IP address and request details as part of serving the page. Cloudflare also runs a small script that hides our email address from spam bots.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface on this site. Your browser fetches the font files from Google, so Google receives your IP address.

We do not use any of these providers to track or profile you.

When you email us

The links on this site that say "Join the waitlist" or show our address open an email to us. We receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to answer you and, if you asked for the waitlist, to tell you when we have room. Our email runs on Google Workspace.

If you are a client

We keep your contact details and what we need to run your service. Payments run through Stripe, our payment processor. Stripe collects your billing details and card number; we never see your full card number. Stripe tells us what was paid so we can keep accurate books.

What our voice assistants collect

Some businesses hire us to run an AI voice assistant that answers their phone. If you call one of those businesses, the assistant may collect your name, phone number, service address, email address, the reason for your call, and anything else you say. Calls may be recorded and transcribed.

The business you called is the operator of that assistant and owns the caller information. Aventra acts as a service provider to that business. To make the assistant work, the call audio and transcript pass through our technology providers: Vapi (voice platform), Twilio (phone lines), Deepgram (speech to text), language models from OpenAI and Anthropic, Make.com (automation) and Google Sheets (where the business's call log lives). Those providers process the data to deliver the service to the business.

If you want a copy of your information from one of those calls, or want it deleted, contact the business you called, or email us at James@aventra-ai.com and we will work with that business to handle your request.
