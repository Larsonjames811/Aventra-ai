Accessibility Statement

Last updated October 10, 2026. Last reviewed October 10, 2026.

We want everyone to be able to read this site and book with us, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification or voice control. If something gets in your way, tell us and we will fix it or get you the same thing another way.

Our target

We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We do not use an accessibility overlay widget. When we find a problem we fix it in the page code.

What we checked on October 10, 2026

  • An automated Lighthouse accessibility scan of the home page and of each legal page.
  • A manual review of the page code for a main landmark and skip link, heading order, page language, text alternatives on images, link text, color contrast and visible keyboard focus.
  • Keyboard operation of the FAQ accordion on the home page, which now reports its open and closed state to assistive technology.

Fixes made in this review: added a skip link and a main landmark, raised the contrast of two low-contrast text elements, added expanded-state announcements to the FAQ buttons, and honored the "reduce motion" browser setting for animations.

Known limitations

  • Booking and payment happen on other companies' pages. Booking uses Cal.com and payment uses Stripe. We cannot change their code. If you have trouble with either, contact us and we will book or invoice you another way.
  • The client logo strip scrolls on its own. It pauses when you hover over it and stops if your browser is set to reduce motion, but it does not yet have a pause button.
  • Some content fades in as you scroll. If your browser is set to reduce motion, it appears without animation.
